The unfinished trail
along Coyote Creek

A paved path is planned from the edge of San Francisco Bay to Anderson Dam in Morgan Hill, about 30 miles in all. After two decades of master plans, grants, floods and false starts, about 21 miles now run without a break from downtown south. The hard part is what's left: about 4.9 miles in north San José, threaded between rail lines, freeways and the creek.

      Estimate the cost of closing the gaps

      The City has never published a cost-to-complete for this trail; its estimate for the whole 67-mile trail network is $300–400 million, with "no defined funding." This calculator applies a cost-per-mile assumption to the remaining mainline gaps.

        This explorer brings together public records: City of San José Council memos and GIS data, Valley Water and County Parks releases, State Coastal Conservancy staff reports, and local journalism. Each event and figure links to its source.
