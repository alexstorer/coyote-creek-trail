# Coyote Creek Trail Explorer

An interactive map, timeline and cost tracker for completing San José's **Coyote Creek Trail**, from the Bay to Anderson Dam.

**Live site:** https://alexstorer.github.io/coyote-creek-trail/

## What's inside

- **Map**: every trail reach from the City of San José's Trail Reach GIS layer, colored by status (open, interim, in design, planned gap), with notes on cost and funding.
- **Timeline**: 43 dated milestones from 1990 to 2035, covering plans, grants, construction, the 2017 flood and encampment clearances. Each one links to its source. It reads like a scrolling story: the year strip stays pinned at the top, and a map beside the cards follows along. For each event, the map flies to where it happened and redraws the trail as it stood on that date.
- **Costs**: total cost and cost per mile for City projects completed 2021–2025, plus a calculator that estimates the cost of closing the remaining ~4.9 miles of gaps.
- **Documents**: master plans, design plan sets, CEQA filings and Council memos. Each one is pinned to the stretch of trail it covers, with a small strip showing where it falls along the trail and a pinned locator map. The section also includes an atlas of 35 figures (`img/schematics/`), ordered north to south. They come from the City's master plans, construction plan sets (35–100%), feasibility studies, Council memos and the Bay Area Ridge Trail maps. One of them is an undated "Coyote Creek Park" long-range plan that imagined swim and boating lagoons along the creek.
- **News**: 34 local articles from 2004 to 2026.
- **Scorecards**: every project event says whether the work was completed, whether it was on schedule (the finish date planned at award vs. the actual opening) and whether it was on budget. Each verdict cites the Council memos. A delivery table in the Costs section summarizes them, and the City's own 2011 cost estimate for the northern gap appears alongside.
- **Photos**: geotagged, openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons. Each one is pinned on the map and attached to the matching timeline events.

## Data sources

- Trail geometry: [City of San José Trail Reach dataset](https://data.sanjoseca.gov/dataset/trail-reach) (ArcGIS layer `OPN_OpenDataService/MapServer/538`), retrieved November 2026. The raw download is in `data/source/`. `data/reaches.geojson` and `data/reaches.js` are trimmed copies. The trimming drops the separate Coyote Alamitos Canal reaches and the duplicate on-street alternate alignments.
- Costs and dates: City Council and committee memos (Legistar), Valley Water releases, State Coastal Conservancy staff reports, and local news. Each one is cited inline in `data/data.js`.
- Location links: `data/geo.js` connects events, documents and plan sheets to trail sections. It also records the year each section opened. Opening years before 2021 are approximate, and the file marks them that way.
- Basemap: Esri Canvas tiles with © OpenStreetMap contributors.

## Caveats

- The gap-closure cost is an extrapolation from recent per-mile costs. It is **not** an official City estimate. The City's only published figure covers the whole trail network: $300–400M, unfunded.
- Reach lengths and opening dates sometimes differ between sources. Where they do, this site uses the City GIS values.

## Updating the site

The facts here go stale. The City opens new segments, Council awards contracts, and Valley Water's floodwalls close parts of the trail. Expect to update the site **at least once a year, after each spring Trail Network annual report**, and whenever one of the open items below resolves. The page shows the date of the last check ("Updated Nov 20, 2026"), which comes from `SITE_UPDATED` in `data/data.js`.

### Where everything lives

| What | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Trail geometry, status, length, opening month | `data/reaches.geojson`, `data/reaches.js` | Generated by `scripts/update_reaches.py`. Don't edit by hand. |
| Timeline events | `TIMELINE` in `data/data.js` | `d` is the date (`YYYY`, `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY-MM-DD`) and also serves as the key everywhere else. Mark expected events `future: true`. |
| Where each event happened, plus its photos and plan sheets | `EVENT_GEO` in `data/geo.js` | Keyed by the event's `d`. |
| Completed / schedule / budget scorecards | `OUTCOMES` in `data/data.js` | Keyed by the event's `d`. Status is one of `good`, `warn`, `bad`, `unk` or `pend`. |
| Project costs (cost charts) | `PROJECTS` in `data/data.js` | |
| Delivery table and 2011 estimate table | `DELIVERY` and `MP_REACHES` in `app.js` | |
| Remaining gaps (cost calculator) | `GAPS` in `data/data.js` | |
| Year each section opened (timeline map) | `BUILT` in `data/geo.js` | Add an entry whenever a section opens. |
| Section popups | `REACH_NOTES` in `data/data.js` | |
| Documents, with their locations and dates | `DOCS` in `data/data.js`; `DOC_GEO` and `DOC_DATES` in `data/geo.js` | All keyed by the exact document title. |
| Plan-sheet atlas | `SCHEMATICS` and `SCH_GROUPS` in `data/geo.js`; images in `img/schematics/` | Images are made with `scripts/make_figure.py`. |
| News, photos, quotes | `NEWS`, `PHOTOS`, `QUOTES` in `data/data.js` | Quotes must be verbatim from the cited article. |
| Intro copy and headline numbers | `index.html` (`#intro` text) and the `stats` array in `app.js`; chapter text in `CHAPTERS` in `data/geo.js` | The "~21 mi", "4.9 mi", "30 miles" and "15 paved miles" figures are typed in by hand. Recheck them after a GIS refresh. |

### Update checklist

1. **Refresh the trail data.**
   ```
   python3 scripts/update_reaches.py
   ```
   The script prints every new, removed or changed section. For each change:
   - When a section opens, add it to `BUILT`, and to `MAINLINE` if it's a new ID. Remove it from `GAPS`, add a timeline event and scorecard, and recheck the intro and headline mileage.
   - When a status changes, for example Planned → Design, update `GAPS` and `REACH_NOTES`.
2. **Check the sources** for anything new since `SITE_UPDATED`:
   - **City Council and committee memos:** search [Legistar](https://sanjose.legistar.com/) for "Coyote Creek Trail" and for project numbers 8087 (Mabury–Empire), 8675 (Phelan–Tully), Watson Park–Julian St, and Five Wounds Trail. The Transportation & Environment Committee's *Bike Plan and Trail Network Annual Report* comes out each March; the latest is in `SRC.te2026`.
   - **City GIS:** the [Trail Reach dataset](https://data.sanjoseca.gov/dataset/trail-reach), covered by step 1.
   - **Valley Water:** [Coyote Creek Flood Protection Project](https://www.valleywater.org/project-updates/creek-river-projects/E1-coyote-creek-flood-protection) updates, which cover trail closures during Phase 2, and the Anderson Dam retrofit.
   - **News:** San José Spotlight, San José Inside, KQED, KTVU, NBC Bay Area, Local News Matters, and Keep Coyote Creek Beautiful.
   - **Photos:** new geotagged files on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Coyote_Creek_Trail). Add them to `PHOTOS` with `ll`, the license and the author.
3. **Close out open items** whose dates have passed:
   - Turn `future: true` events into real ones or move their dates.
   - Change `pend` scorecards to their outcomes.
   - Replace "Final cost not published" if a closeout memo reports one.
4. **Add documents and plan sheets.** Add new PDFs to `DOCS`, with `DOC_GEO` and `DOC_DATES`. To feature a drawing or map from one, cut it out with
   ```
   python3 scripts/make_figure.py path/to/file.pdf PAGE key [--crop x0 y0 x1 y1]
   ```
   then add the result to `SCHEMATICS` and `SCH_GROUPS`. Keep the source PDFs outside `site/`; only the cropped images are published.
5. **Validate.**
   ```
   node scripts/check_data.js
   ```
   This must print `OK`. It catches events, documents, photos, plan sheets and sections that point at nothing. Warnings list documents that still have no date or location.
6. **Look at it.** Run `python3 -m http.server` and check the intro chapters, the timeline (scroll it and click the filters), the map popups and the Costs section, on desktop and on a phone-sized window.
7. **Stamp and publish.**
   ```
   python3 scripts/stamp.py          # sets SITE_UPDATED to today and bumps the ?v= asset stamps
   git commit -am "Update: <what changed>" && git push
   ```
   GitHub Pages redeploys in about a minute.

### Open items to watch (as of Nov 20, 2026)

- **Watson Park–Julian St:** 35% design was due in summer 2026. Look for its completion and for the next phase's funding (Measure B Call for Funding 4).
- **Valley Water floodwall Phase 2** (Aug 2026–Oct 2028): temporary closures of the Mabury–Watson Park section and of Selma Olinder and William St parks.
- **Five Wounds Trail and the 1922 Senter Rd trestle:** whether the trestle is kept or demolished, and the planning for the I-280–Santa Clara St section, due Dec 2027.
- **Final project costs** for William–Story, Story–Phelan, Phelan–Tully and the Singleton interim bridge, all unpublished so far.
- **Ten documents with unverified dates,** listed as warnings by `check_data.js`. Their PDFs couldn't be fetched automatically. Download them in a browser to date them and pin them to the map.
- **Anderson Dam retrofit** (about 2027–2034): watch for closures near the trail's south end.

## Running locally

This is a static site with no build step. To view it locally:

```
python3 -m http.server
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.
